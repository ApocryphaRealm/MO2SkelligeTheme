# Third-party notices - Skellige MO2 Theme

No third-party files are included.

## The Witcher 3

The frame round the highlighted row follows the shape of the frame The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt draws round its menu entries.
It is drawn by `tools\build_theme.py`; no game files are used.

## Apocrypha Menu Framework, Njordlinger MO2 Theme

The frame art (`src\amf-art\`) is Apocrypha Menu Framework's, and the icons (`src\icons\`) are the Njordlinger MO2
Theme's, recoloured - both by the same author (ApocryphaRealm, GPL-3.0-or-later). The stylesheet began as the Oathvein MO2
Theme's (same author), whose shape language came from Oathvein UI by Nithog
(https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/160916) through AMF's Oathvein theme; no files of Oathvein UI are used.

## Mod Organizer 2

Widget selectors and the toolbar action names come from Mod Organizer 2 (https://github.com/ModOrganizer2/modorganizer, GPL-3.0).
